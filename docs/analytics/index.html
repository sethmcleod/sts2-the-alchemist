About these stats

Where they come from
When a run ends, the game can upload an anonymous summary of it. It only does this when Upload Data is on in the game's settings. The Alchemist also has its own switch, Share Anonymous Run Analytics, in the mod settings. Nothing sent includes your name or Steam ID.
Which runs count
Standard mode runs that got past floor 5. Abandoned runs and runs with the full console on are left out.
Reading the ranges
A win rate from a handful of runs can swing a lot. The thin line on a bar shows where the real rate most likely sits (a 95% interval), so a wide line means there is not much data yet.
Why card win rates run high
Winning runs last longer and finish with more cards, so the typical card wins more often than the overall win rate. That is why cards are compared with their peers.
